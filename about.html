Thanks for stopping by!

Design found me at the intersection of two things I loved (and still do!): making things thoughtfully, and making things that solve real problems for people.

I’m a Product Designer who helps teams figure out what to build in complex product spaces. I’m most drawn to the messy middle of product work: when the problem is still taking shape, information is fragmented, and the team needs to build confidence in what to do next.

Across my work, that has meant getting close to customers before a solution is clear, connecting signals across teams and systems, and staying close enough to the details that the final experience still reflects the thinking behind it.

Outside of work, you can find me outdoors, eating my way through a new city, making very tiny fruits, or on the hunt for a good coffee shop. Originally from sunny Hong Kong, I've lived across Pittsburgh, London, and am now based in Singapore.
