Thanks for stopping by!

Design found me at the intersection of two things I loved: making beautiful, well thought out things that solved problems that mattered to real people. Five years in, that's still what keeps me excited.

I'm a Product Designer at Meta, where I've spent my career working on complex, foundational products. Over time, I've realised I'm most energised by problems that don't come neatly packaged: the ones that are fragmented, ambiguous, and require connecting information from many places before a path forward becomes clear.

When I'm not designing, you can find me outdoors, eating my way through a new city, making very tiny fruits, or on the hunt for a good coffee shop. Originally from sunny Hong Kong, I've lived across Pittsburgh, London, and am now based in Singapore.
